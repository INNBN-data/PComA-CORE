# Ethics Statement and Contacts

## Institutional Review Board Statement

This study was conducted in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki and approved by the Ethics Committee of the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Neurovascular Disease (Research Project No. 4623; approval date: 25 May 2026).

## Informed Consent Statement

Patient consent was waived because of the retrospective study design, minimal risk to participants, and use of de-identified data.

## Data Availability Statement

The patient-level analytical data are not publicly distributed because of institutional, ethical, and data-governance requirements. Access may be considered for qualified researchers following approval by the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Neurovascular Disease, applicable ethics review, and execution of an appropriate data-use agreement.

## Code Availability Statement

The analytical code, reference data dictionary, model specifications, and reproducibility documentation are contained in this repository. Exact numerical reproduction requires the locked de-identified analytical dataset and original analysis objects.
