# Reproducibility Notes

- No synthetic patient rows or synthetic fallback are present.
- The manuscript is authoritative whenever code, metadata, or secondary artefacts conflict with it.
- The development and temporal cohorts are fixed at 1997–2020 and 2021–2026.
- Learned preprocessing is fitted only within development data and, during resampling, only within each training fold.
- Patient grouping is mandatory for all resampling.
- Outcome values are never imputed.
- `results/reported_metrics.yaml` stores locked manuscript targets and is not generated by model scripts.
- Reproduced outputs are written to `results/generated/` and can be compared against the locked targets.
- Exact equality of AUCs, confidence intervals, calibration parameters, and figures requires the original resampling assignments, model objects, and prediction files.
